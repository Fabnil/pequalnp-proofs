# Operand sides for PG(2,4)

The Lean theorem `PlaneCoverReadSets.planeFourPairSides` gives a necessary condition for [L-meta-8101](https://pequalnp.com/desk/row?gap=L-meta-8101). A family satisfying its disjoint operand and read conditions and representing all minimal vertex covers must have at least four pairwise vertex-disjoint operand sides, each consisting of one point and one line. Lean checks the finite facts about the incidence graph and the general argument. The theorem uses `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.

In a regular bipartite graph of degree \(d\) with at most one common neighbor between distinct vertices, take fewer than \(d\) vertices out of one color class and add their neighbors. This gives a minimal cover. Each kept vertex has a neighbor outside the union of the neighborhoods of the removed vertices. Each added vertex has a removed neighbor. In particular, each whole color class is a minimal cover.

For a vertex \(v\), start with the opposite color class and remove one neighbor of \(v\). For each of the \(d\) choices the cover must contain an operand side containing \(v\). If \(v\) occurs in fewer than \(d\) sides, two choices reuse the same side. Its only vertex of the color of \(v\) is \(v\), because the two removed vertices have only \(v\) in common in their neighborhoods. The single-color cover also guarantees a side containing \(v\) and wholly contained in its own color class. A singleton side can meet both conditions.

Assign each vertex to a side containing it as the only vertex of its color. Give singletons priority. A reused side is exactly a point-line pair, and its two vertices have no singleton side. The pure sides for those two vertices lie outside the image of the assignment, one of each color. With 42 vertices and 40 sides, at least four sides must therefore be shared by two assigned vertices. Distinct shared sides are disjoint because each vertex is assigned once.

The disjointness of the **selected** slots is a substantive hypothesis. The following construction satisfies the relaxed independent-side coverage condition, both read bounds, disjointness of the two sides of each slot, and edge coverage. It uses the same difference set as the card. For \(0\le r<7\), put \(p_i=r+7i\) and \(\ell_i=r+7((i+2)\bmod 3)\) for \(0\le i<3\), using local line labels \(0,\dots,20\). These six vertices induce a 6-cycle with \(p_i\) incident to \(\ell_j\) exactly when \(i\ne j\). The seven cycles partition the vertex set.

Use the pair of full color classes in one slot. For each cycle, put the three points and the three lines in the two sides of one slot. Pack the 21 remaining sides \(\{p_i,\ell_i\}\), together with three empty sides, into 12 slots. There are 20 slots and each vertex occurs in at most three. Every edge crosses the first slot. In any vertex cover, a vertex whose paired vertex is present belongs to a contained two-vertex side. If its paired vertex is absent, the other two vertices of its color in the cycle are forced into the cover, so its pure three-vertex side is contained. Thus every cover is a union of contained sides when both sides of a slot may be used. L-meta-8101 still requires mutually exclusive choices at each slot.
